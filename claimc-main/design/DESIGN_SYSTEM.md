Color palette

Primary (ink/black)

#0A0A0A – header nav bar, footer bar, primary buttons ("Start quote", "Download app"), stat numbers, headline text
Used as the dominant "anchor" color — dark bookends (header + footer) framing a light body

Neutral / background

#F7F4ED (warm off-white/cream) – default section background (intro section, quick-access section, testimonial section)
#FFFFFF – card surfaces (agent card, account cards, product cards)
#EDE9E0 – secondary tinted neutral used for the final CTA section

Secondary accent

#E7ECDF (muted sage green) – full-section background for "Insurance for your car and home…"
Three pastel accent chips inside the intro cards: lavender 
#E6DFF4, mint 
#DCE9DA, blush 
#F6E1D8 — each pairs with a small line-icon, used only for that one card row (a soft color-coding system rather than brand colors)

Text colors

Heading: 
#111111 near-black, high contrast
Body/secondary text: 
#6B6A62 warm gray — used for subcopy, captions, stat labels, nav-adjacent text
Text on dark surfaces (header/footer/hero): 
#FFFFFF, with a muted 
#B8B8B0 for footer link labels/copyright

Borders

Hairline 1px solid #E3E0D6 — input fields, card outlines, footer column dividers
No heavy borders or shadows; separation comes mostly from background-color contrast (cream vs. white vs. green) rather than strokes
Photography usage

Photography is used sparingly, in exactly two places, everything else is icon/illustration + flat color:

Hero background — full-bleed lifestyle photo (family outdoors, warm/golden tone) with a dark gradient overlay at the top for nav legibility
Mobile app section — a professional/lifestyle photo (woman using phone) with a phone-mockup screenshot overlaid on top

Every other section (product cards, account cards, stat block, testimonial) is pure typography + flat pastel/neutral fields + simple line icons — no photography.

Typography

Font family: a single grotesk-style sans-serif across the whole page (bold weight for headings, regular/medium for body) — no serif, no secondary display face.

Type scale (approx.):

Role	Size	Weight
Hero H1	44–52px	Bold
Section H2	30–34px	Bold
Stat numbers	34–38px	Bold
Card heading (Vehicle/Home/Business)	18–20px	Semibold
Body / paragraph	15–16px	Regular
Nav links / buttons	14px	Medium/Semibold
Small labels (stat captions, footer links)	12–13px	Regular

Line-height is generous on paragraphs (~1.6) and tight on headlines (~1.1–1.15).

Border radius
Buttons: fully pill-shaped (border-radius: 999px)
Cards (agent card, product cards, account cards): large rounding, ~16–20px
Input field: medium rounding, ~8–10px
Phone mockup image: large rounding, ~24px+
No sharp/0-radius elements anywhere — the whole system uses soft, consistent rounding2
